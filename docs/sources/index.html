Everything on this site is derived from public documents. This page says where each one came from, and on what terms it is used.

Species at Risk Public Registry

NB-SAR and AC-SAR are derived from recovery strategies, action plans and management plans published in the Species at Risk Public Registry by Environment and Climate Change Canada, Parks Canada and Fisheries and Oceans Canada.

Information from these publications is reproduced here for non-commercial purposes. It derives from copies of official works published by the Government of Canada, and this site has not been produced in affiliation with, or with the endorsement of, the Government of Canada.

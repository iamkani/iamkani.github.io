## Species at Risk Public Registry

NB-SAR and AC-SAR are derived from recovery strategies, action plans and management plans published in the [Species at Risk Public Registry](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/species-risk-public-registry.html) by Environment and Climate Change Canada, Parks Canada and Fisheries and Oceans Canada.

Information from these publications is reproduced here for non-commercial purposes. It derives from copies of official works published by the Government of Canada, and **this site has not been produced in affiliation with, or with the endorsement of, the Government of Canada.**
